02 / A BETTER FIRST TRY

Good cookies. Good experiments.

01

Change one thing.

Try one swap at a time before combining them. You'll know what made your cookie softer, thicker, or more tender.

02

Give it a moment.

Whole grains need time to hydrate. Rest dough for 20–30 minutes before deciding whether it needs extra liquid.

03

Bake a test pair.

Bake two cookies first. Check spread and edges, then adjust the remaining dough. Your oven gets the final say.

Nutrition assumptions & the base batch +

This illustrative batch uses 240 g all-purpose flour, 170 g butter, 150 g granulated sugar, 50 g egg, and 150 g semisweet chocolate chips, plus 4 g vanilla, 3 g baking soda, and 3 g salt. Tiny contributions from vanilla and leavening are excluded. It is an experimental formula, not a tested recipe.
